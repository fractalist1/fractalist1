## About

Fractalist is an independent financial market analytics project founded and developed by Mohammad Farid Kaviani. It is designed to help users explore market data, inspect charts across multiple timeframes, organize symbols, and build clearer analytical workflows.

## What we are building

- A responsive charting interface for desktop and mobile
- Searchable multi-market instrument catalogs
- Historical and real-time candle delivery through our own server-side datafeed adapters
- Multi-timeframe market analysis and research workflows
- A foundation for watchlists, formulas, filters, and alerts

## Project status

Fractalist is publicly accessible and under active development. The product supplies its own market data through custom backend integrations and does not provide trade execution or investment advice.
